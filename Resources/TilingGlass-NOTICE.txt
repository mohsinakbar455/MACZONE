MACZONE's 2026-10-08 drag repair adapts concepts and implementation from:
  TilingGlass/Input/DragMonitor.swift
  TilingGlass/Input/DragCoordinator.swift
  TilingGlass/WindowDriver/AccessibilityElement.swift
  Packages/TilingCore/Sources/TilingCore/ZoneHitTesting.swift

Affected MACZONE files:
  Sources/MACZONE/DragState.swift
  Sources/MACZONE/WindowEngine.swift

Adaptations include window-at-pointer AX lookup, permission-aware input
monitoring, modifier-change re-evaluation during a drag, confirmed window
movement, inclusive hit testing, and anchor-to-hover rectangular spanning.
MACZONE retains its own layouts, display/Space bindings, preferences, overlay,
edge-snapping behavior, and window-moving implementation. Drop handling also
re-evaluates the final pointer and modifiers. The deterministic tests exercise
the same WindowDragState used by the native event driver.

The reference project is not bundled as a separate application or dependency.
No TilingGlass installation, startup registration, or settings are modified.
